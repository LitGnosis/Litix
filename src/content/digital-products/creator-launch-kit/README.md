# Creator Launch Kit
## The 30-day launch planning system

**Version:** 1.0 draft  
**Format:** Editable worksheets (Markdown/Google Docs friendly)  
**Intended user:** Independent creators, small brands, and solo operators

## Welcome

This kit turns a launch idea into a series of manageable decisions. Work through the sections in order, but keep the plan lightweight: a useful launch plan is one you can actually follow.

### The clarity test

Finish this sentence in plain language:

> I help [audience] move from [current problem] toward [desired outcome] with [offer / approach].

If this sentence is hard to explain, simplify the offer before adding more marketing.

## 3. Launch readiness checklist

### Offer
- [ ] Offer and deliverables are clearly described.
- [ ] Price, terms, and refund policy are decided.
- [ ] The purchase or signup path has been tested.
- [ ] Delivery instructions are ready.
- [ ] Support contact and response expectations are clear.

### Content
- [ ] Core message and CTA are consistent.
- [ ] Images and copy are approved for use.
- [ ] Links and tracking parameters are checked.
- [ ] Accessibility: captions, readable text, and alt text are included where applicable.
- [ ] Claims are accurate and evidence-backed.

### Operations
- [ ] Owner and backup owner are assigned.
- [ ] Launch-day monitoring window is scheduled.
- [ ] Known risks and fallback plan are written down.
- [ ] Metrics baseline is recorded.
- [ ] Post-launch review is on the calendar.

## 6. Launch-day runbook

**Before publishing**
- [ ] Confirm the destination page loads on mobile and desktop.
- [ ] Test the main CTA and all important links.
- [ ] Confirm offer details, terms, and availability.
- [ ] Have support responses and escalation owner ready.
- [ ] Record the starting metrics.

**During the launch window**
- [ ] Publish the planned announcement.
- [ ] Verify it appears correctly on each channel.
- [ ] Monitor replies, support, and checkout/signup health.
- [ ] Record issues without making unverified promises.
- [ ] Pause promotion if a payment, privacy, or delivery issue appears.

**After the window**
- [ ] Record outcomes and notable questions.
- [ ] Resolve outstanding customer requests.
- [ ] Capture lessons while they are fresh.

### Conversion formula

Conversion rate = conversions ÷ qualified visits × 100.

Use consistent definitions and time windows. Do not compare metrics across platforms as if they were measured identically.

## 8. Retrospective

- What worked better than expected?
- Where did people get confused?
- Which audience questions came up repeatedly?
- Which content created meaningful conversations or qualified visits?
- What broke or created unnecessary work?
- What should be repeated, stopped, or tested next?
- What one change would make the next launch simpler?

**The rule:** use the results to improve the next iteration; don't treat one campaign as a guaranteed predictor of future outcomes.
