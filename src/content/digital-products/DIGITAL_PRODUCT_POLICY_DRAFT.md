# Litix Digital Products — Policy Draft (Not Final)

**Status:** Internal draft. This is not legal advice and must not be published as final policy without review and completion of the missing business details.

## Product description and access

Each product page should list the actual files, formats, compatibility notes, license terms, and any limitations before purchase. Customers must know exactly what they receive. Do not promise audience growth, revenue, or campaign results.

## License (draft concept)

Unless a product-specific license states otherwise, the proposed license would permit the purchasing individual or organization to use and modify the purchased templates for its own business and client work, subject to the final approved terms. It would not permit reselling, redistributing, sublicensing, or publishing the source files as a competing template product. The final license must define team seats, client deliverables, and transfer rights clearly.

## Delivery

After the payment provider confirms the transaction, Litix would provide a receipt and a time-limited download method. Customers should be able to request a new link after identity/order verification. Links must not expose paid files publicly.

## Refunds and access revocation

The final policy must clearly state how refund requests are handled for digital files, how exceptions are evaluated, how chargebacks affect access, and how customers can contact support. Any policy must comply with applicable law and payment-provider rules. Do not use a blanket “no refunds” statement without legal review.

## Customer support

Before accepting purchases, provide a monitored support email or contact method, expected response window, order lookup process, and a recovery process for failed or expired downloads.

## Details to finalize before sale

- Legal business identity and customer-facing contact details.
- Customer support email and response-time target.
- Final product license and team/client-use boundaries.
- Refund window, eligibility, and exceptions.
- Applicable tax handling and invoices/receipts.
- Privacy/data retention and payment-provider disclosure.
- Product formats, compatibility, version updates, and access expiry.
