# Agency Starter Pack
## Client onboarding and repeatable delivery workflows

**Version:** 1.0 draft  
**Format:** Editable checklists, briefs, and reporting templates  
**Intended user:** Small agencies, freelancers, and independent consultants  
**License:** Draft product content; final customer license still needs approval

## Welcome

This pack helps a small agency deliver work consistently from first conversation through final handoff. Adapt each workflow to the services you actually provide. It is an operating aid, not legal, tax, or financial advice.

## 2. Discovery call agenda (30–45 minutes)

1. Context and goals — 5 min
2. Audience and current customer journey — 10 min
3. Existing activity and what has been learned — 10 min
4. Constraints, resources, approvals, and timeline — 10 min
5. Recap, open questions, and next steps — 5 min

### Discovery notes
- What does success mean to the client?
- How is the current baseline measured?
- What has already been tried?
- What evidence supports the current hypothesis?
- What is in scope?
- What is explicitly out of scope?
- Who approves work and how quickly?
- What could delay delivery?
- What access or data is needed?
- What would make the engagement not worthwhile?

## 4. Access and asset checklist

Request only the access needed for the agreed work. Prefer role-based access, least privilege, and client-owned accounts. Never ask clients to send passwords in plain text.

- [ ] Brand guidelines and approved logo files
- [ ] Approved imagery, video, and usage rights
- [ ] Existing content and campaign examples
- [ ] Relevant audience / performance exports
- [ ] Analytics access with appropriate role
- [ ] Social account access via official permissions
- [ ] Landing page / website contact
- [ ] Tracking links and conversion definitions
- [ ] Brand voice and prohibited claims
- [ ] Approval contacts and escalation path
- [ ] Secure access handoff method
- [ ] Access review / revocation date

## 5. Weekly delivery checklist

### Plan
- [ ] Review priorities and dependencies.
- [ ] Confirm what is due and who owns it.
- [ ] Identify blocked items and escalate early.

### Produce
- [ ] Use the approved brief and source assets.
- [ ] Check brand, accuracy, links, and rights.
- [ ] Check mobile layout and accessibility where applicable.
- [ ] Verify that claims and metrics are supported.

### Approve and deliver
- [ ] Send work to the correct approver.
- [ ] Record approval and requested revisions.
- [ ] Confirm delivery links and permissions.
- [ ] Update status and next steps.
- [ ] Record decisions that affect scope or dates.

## 8. Project closeout and handoff

- [ ] Deliverables match agreed acceptance criteria.
- [ ] Final files are organized and access permissions checked.
- [ ] Client knows where assets and documentation live.
- [ ] Outstanding tasks and exclusions are documented.
- [ ] Final report and lessons learned are delivered.
- [ ] Invoices / account items are handled per agreement.
- [ ] Access is reduced or revoked when no longer required.
- [ ] Client-owned credentials remain with the client.
- [ ] Feedback or testimonial request is optional and consent-based.
- [ ] Internal retrospective is completed.

### Retrospective
- What went well?
- Where did we lose time?
- What changed after kickoff?
- Were approvals timely and clear?
- What should become a reusable process?
- What would we change in the next engagement?

## 9. Operating principles

- Keep client data confidential and use least-privilege access.
- Get written approval for scope changes.
- Be transparent about delays, assumptions, and risks.
- Do not fabricate performance numbers or testimonials.
- Do not promise outcomes outside your control.
- Use written agreements for scope, payment, IP, confidentiality, and termination.
