# Litix Digital Products — Internal Source Assets

These are working source files for the first three planned digital kits. They are not currently customer downloads and should not be placed in a public download directory until the license and delivery model are finalized.

## Creator Launch Kit
- `creator-launch-kit/README.md`: launch brief, audience map, readiness checklist, 30-day launch rhythm, content brief, launch-day runbook, scorecard, retrospective.
- `creator-launch-kit/30-day-content-planner.csv`: editable 30-day planner.

## Social Growth Toolkit
- `social-growth-toolkit/README.md`: baseline, audience signal log, campaign brief, weekly planner, experiment cards, review, monthly dashboard, ethical guardrails.
- `social-growth-toolkit/experiment-tracker.csv`: reusable experiment log.
- `social-growth-toolkit/monthly-dashboard.csv`: spreadsheet-ready metrics dashboard.

## Agency Starter Pack
- `agency-starter-pack/README.md`: client intake, discovery, scope, access checklist, delivery, reporting, decision log, handoff, retrospective.
- `agency-starter-pack/client-project-tracker.csv`: reusable delivery tracker.
- `agency-starter-pack/client-status-report.csv`: reporting worksheet.
