## Welcome

This toolkit helps you make social growth work more deliberate. It does not promise a specific follower count or engagement result. Use it to set a baseline, run clear experiments, and learn what is useful for your own audience.

### KPI selection

Pick one primary metric that reflects the goal:
- Awareness: qualified reach or relevant video views.
- Engagement: meaningful comments, saves, shares, or platform-defined engagement.
- Traffic: qualified link clicks or landing-page visits.
- Leads: completed lead forms or qualified inquiries.
- Sales: verified purchases or revenue.

Avoid optimizing for a metric that looks good but does not serve the campaign objective.

## 9. Ethical growth guardrails

- Do not buy fake engagement or misrepresent audience size.
- Do not use spam, deceptive giveaways, or misleading claims.
- Respect platform rules, privacy, consent, and intellectual property.
- Do not promise guaranteed growth outcomes.
- Treat audience data as confidential and limit access to people who need it.
